Our Purpose & Ethos

A Music App Built for Listeners

Discover the philosophy, engineering, and privacy commitments behind Melodyx.

Why We Built Melodyx

Mainstream music apps have become cluttered with audio advertisements, paywalls, and aggressive algorithmic pushes. Users are frequently prevented from playing the exact song they requested without a monthly fee.

Melodyx was designed from first principles: a lightweight, ad-free music application for Android that provides high-bitrate 320kbps audio, respect for user autonomy, and open playlist portability.

Universal Playlist Portability

Your music belongs to you, not to a subscription platform. Through our built-in Universal Playlist Importer, you can paste links from Spotify, JioSaavn, YouTube, and Apple Music to immediately import and play your favorite collections with full background playback.

Privacy & Data Ethics

Melodyx does not sell user data, serve commercial advertising, or inject tracking SDKs. Sign-in with your Google account is used solely to securely synchronize your personal library and liked songs across your devices.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Melodyx really 100% free?

Yes. Melodyx has no subscription fees, no locked playback limits, and no audio advertisements.

Which Android versions are supported?

Melodyx is compatible with Android 8.0 (Oreo) and above, including Android 9, 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, and 15.

When is the Web Player launching?

The Melodyx Web Player is actively in development. You can preview free Creative Commons tracks on our Music Library page today, and the full streaming web app will be available soon.
